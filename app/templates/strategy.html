🚀 4 Core Pillars of CTR Optimization Methodology

🎯

1. Target Encoding & Smoothing

Replaces high-cardinality string IDs (site_id, app_id, device_ip) with out-of-fold historical click probabilities. Empirical Bayes smoothing prevents noise on rare IDs:

S_i = (count_i · mean_i + m · global_mean) / (count_i + m)
⏰

2. Temporal Dynamics & Cyclical Hours

Decomposes the 11-day timestamp format (YYMMDDHH) into hour_of_day and day_of_week, applying sine/cosine cyclical encoding to capture morning vs evening peak conversion windows.

🌐

3. Field-Aware Factorization (FFM)

Learns dedicated latent vectors for every feature pair (e.g. device_type $\times$ app_category), capturing critical pairwise interactions essential for sparse ad impression click prediction.

⚡

4. Model Blending & Stacking

Combines predicted click probabilities from Logistic Regression (linear baseline) with Gradient Boosting (non-linear decision trees) via weighted rank-averaging to reduce variance.
