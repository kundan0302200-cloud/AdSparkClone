📊 Evaluation Metrics Dictionary Metric Definitions

📉 Logarithmic Loss (Log Loss)

Primary Kaggle competition metric measuring the uncertainty of probabilistic predictions. Formula:

Loss = -1/N Σ [ y · ln(p) + (1-y) · ln(1-p) ]

Lower values indicate superior calibration and lower uncertainty.

📈 ROC-AUC Score

Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve. Evaluates the model's ability to rank ad impressions by click likelihood across all classification thresholds.

0.50 = Random Guessing | 1.00 = Perfect Ranking Discrimination.

🎯 Accuracy & F1-Score

Classification accuracy measures overall correct predictions (Click vs No Click) at decision threshold 0.5. F1-Score balances Precision and Recall on the rare Click class.

📏 Continuous Regression Metrics

For linear regression continuous click predictions, evaluates Mean Squared Error (MSE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), Mean Absolute Error (MAE), and $R^2$ Score.
