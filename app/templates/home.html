📌 What is AdSpark? Core Objective

In digital advertising ecosystems (Real-Time Bidding, sponsored search, and display ad exchanges), Click-Through Rate (CTR) measures the ratio of ad clicks to total impressions. Accurately estimating CTR enables ad networks to display relevant ads to users while maximizing publisher revenue and advertiser return on ad spend (ROAS).

🎯 Real-Time Ad Bidding

Predicts click probabilities in milliseconds to dynamically price ad impressions during live RTB auctions.

📱 Multi-Device Analytics

Analyzes impression signals across mobile devices, banner slots, temporal hours, and high-cardinality app IDs.

📉 Log Loss Optimization

Evaluates models strictly on Logarithmic Loss to penalize confident false predictions and output calibrated probabilities.

Dataset Ingestion
