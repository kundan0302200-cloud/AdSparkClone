🧠 Why Logistic Regression Wins for AdSpark Production Insights

⚡ Sub-Millisecond Speed

Real-time bidding (RTB) auctions mandate response times under 10ms. Linear dot products complete predictions in under 0.1ms per impression.

🎯 Calibrated Probabilities

The sigmoid activation outputs smoothly calibrated probabilities, avoiding extreme 0 or 1 overconfidence that heavily inflates Log Loss penalties.

🛡️ Overfitting Resistance

Unlike single deep decision trees (Log Loss 1.8510), linear models do not over-split sparse One-Hot categorical noise.
